How HHH Is Run

A small team does the paperwork of a big organization here, because our structure was designed for it from day one. Every stage of our work has exactly one owner, and every decision, dollar, and deadline goes into one ledger the moment it happens. Nothing counts as done until it is verified.

Chairman and Chief of Staff

Irish, Chairman and founder. Irish sets our direction, chairs our board, and holds final authority on every spend, launch, and legal call.
Nova, Chief of Staff. Nova is Irish's AI partner. She runs our boardroom, coordinates our departments, keeps our ledger, and verifies our work before anything is called done.

The AI Boardroom and our departments

Our boardroom seats cover imagination, marketing, sales, finance, and legal review, and they work as checklists on every spend, launch, and legal question before it moves. Our working departments cover ideas, production, quality checks, legal screening, launches, money, land, promotion, and daily administration, each with one owner per stage.

This is not decoration. Our own bylaws authorize it by name. Article III, Section 3.3 of the HHH Foundation bylaws authorizes the board to integrate the AI Boardroom for administration, data logging, grant writing, and logistical planning, under human executive oversight. Irish chairs. The machine does the paperwork. The people do the work that matters.

Our structure

Hanna Enterprises LLC will hold our land titles and deeds as the umbrella over HHH, so the foundation and its people stay protected as we grow. Members who want a stake in the growth belong on the LLC side, and that structure goes through a full legal review with our counsel before any share is offered to anyone. We do it right and we do it legal, from day one.

Our living facilities will include men's facilities and women's facilities, and separate facilities for our gay and trans residents alongside them, so everyone has a space where they are comfortable. Our rules are simple, they are known up front, and they do not bend. Everybody gets the basics, and people who will not live right with everybody else can be removed.

Formation status. HHH is in formation. We are not yet a registered nonprofit, and our filings are in progress through counsel. We do not accept donations through this site, and nothing given to HHH at this stage should be treated as a tax deductible gift.
