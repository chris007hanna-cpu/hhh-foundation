Ways to Give

Every dollar goes directly to our people: housing, food, power, tools, and training, paid at the source by the foundation as it forms.

Read this first, because we do this in the open. HHH is in formation and is not yet a registered nonprofit. Until foundation accounts exist, giving runs through Irish's own payment handles, listed on this page only. Gifts are not tax deductible. Anyone else claiming to collect money for HHH, on any app or any page, is not us.

Time, skills, and physical resources

Volunteer Your Time. Cleanup crews, kitchen help, driving, and plain showing up. Our work lanes are listed on the Get Involved page.
Skilled Professional Services. Building trades, repair work, legal and paperwork help, and mentoring. Skills keep our money pointed at people instead of contractors.
Material and Equipment Donations. Tools, building materials, tents, and gear go straight to work in our community. Offer what you have through the Get Involved page.
