Get Involved With HHH

HHH is being built in the open, and there is room in it for anyone who wants to help people land on their feet. Here is where each kind of help fits, in this formation stage.

Follow our formation

Our filings are in progress through counsel, and this site is our public home while we form. As our status changes, this page changes with it, in plain language. Check back here for formation news, our first land steps, and the opening of our work lanes.

Offer land or property

Land is our foundation in the literal sense. If you hold a parcel you cannot keep, a house you cannot fix, or an estate you want to leave to work that matters, talk to us before the taxes and penalties bury it. We research every parcel carefully, we kill any parcel carrying association dues, and we put donated land to work for people, with the paperwork done right through counsel.

Join a work lane

Cleanup and rehab crews. Trash and crime corridor cleanups around town, and hands on work fixing abandoned houses into homes.
Vending and markets. Hot dog carts at the convention center and downtown, plus jewelry, crafts, and clothing at our market lanes.
Camo Santa. Holiday kettle ringing in camouflage Santa costumes, raising money for HHH and for veterans.
Skills of every kind. Building, cooking, driving, organizing, paperwork, and plain showing up. If you can do it, we can use it.

Bless a person directly

Our plan includes a supplies list where people name what they need, tents, gear, and basics, and neighbors bless specific people directly, with a bartering system alongside it where folks trade what they have for what they need. That page opens here once our formation and our accounts are real. Until then, we publish no donation links and no payment handles, on purpose. Anyone claiming to collect money for HHH today is not us.

Formation status. HHH is in formation. We are not yet a registered nonprofit, and our filings are in progress through counsel. We do not accept donations through this site, and nothing given to HHH at this stage should be treated as a tax deductible gift.
